Added a `dedicated_picker` option to the Decluttering Selector card. By default, Add Card still shows one "Decluttering: `<template name>`" entry per template, unchanged. Set `dedicated_picker: true` to replace all of those with a single "Decluttering: Choose a Template" entry instead, sorted to the top of the picker's community-cards list — its editor shows a searchable, live-previewed grid of every template on the dashboard, so you can filter and pick one without scrolling a row per template.
